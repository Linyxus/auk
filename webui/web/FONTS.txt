Bundled web fonts — attribution and licenses

without relying on fonts installed on the viewer's machine. Each is licensed
under the SIL Open Font License v1.1; the full license text for each is included
alongside it here and travels with the fonts in every distribution (including
the packaged `dist/auk` binary).
